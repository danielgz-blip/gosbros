# GOSBROS. Brand Voice & Copywriting Guide

## 1. Core Philosophy & The Soviet Bus Stop Metaphor
GOSBROS. provides highly functional, precision-engineered architecture and visual identities without the luxury price tag. Our overarching metaphor is the **Soviet Bus Stop**: structures designed strictly as public utilities, but crafted with extraordinary aesthetic ambition and structural beauty.
- **Functional yet Beautiful (Funcional pero hermoso)**
- **A socialist proposal for business (Una propuesta de negocio socialista)**: World-class design is a public utility, a baseline right, not an exclusive commodity for the wealthy.

## 2. Tone & Character (Brutal Honesty)
- **Confident & Direct**: Say exactly what you mean. Do not use corporate jargon or marketing fluff.
- **Anti-Snob Brutalism**: Expose the efficiency. Show that high standards are achieved through technical rigor and hard work, not through bloated agency overhead or elitism.
- **Mechanical & Transparent**: Like the visible grid lines on our website and exposed concrete in our architecture, our words should reveal their structure.

### Examples:
- *Avoid*: "We curate bespoke luxury experiences for discerning clients."
- *Use*: "High efficiency. How refreshing. / Alta eficiencia. Qué refrescante."
- *Avoid*: "World-class design for premium brands."
- *Use*: "Elite standard, honest pricing. / Alto estándar, costo justo."

## 3. Bilingual Rules (Spanish & English)
Since we are based in Mexico City, **Spanish is our primary operational language**, and English is our secondary language for international visibility.
- **Translation Philosophy**: Do not translate word-for-word. Translate the *punchiness* and *attitude*. If a literal translation sounds weak, rewrite it to maintain the brutalist rhythm.
- **Caps Lock / Typography**: When writing section headers (like in the Hero or Archive), utilize ALL CAPS to match the massive structural typography of our design system.

## 4. Vocabulary / Word Bank
- **Architecture**: Concrete, structure, utility, precision, modular, efficiency, low-cost, execution. / Concreto, estructura, utilidad, precisión, modular, eficiencia, bajo costo, ejecución.
- **Design/Branding**: Contrast, logic, grid, identity, radical, system. / Contraste, lógica, retícula, identidad, radical, sistema.
- **Value**: Transparent, honest, standard, right. / Transparente, honesto, estándar, derecho.
